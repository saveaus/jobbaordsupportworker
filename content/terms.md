{Site name} is a job board for support work in Australia. Applicants use the site free. Providers pay $249 a month plus GST, or $2,490 a year plus GST, after a 14-day free trial. A card is required to start the trial. Cancel anytime.

## Employers only

You must be the employer for the jobs you post. Recruitment and labour-hire agencies are not allowed on this plan. Agency? Contact us at {contact email}. We may suspend accounts that post for other businesses.

## Listings

Jobs expire after 30 days. You can mark a job filled at any time. The first job from a new provider is held for approval; later jobs publish instantly.

## Imported listings

We may show a publicly advertised job as a link-out. Claim it free if it is yours. To have a listing removed, email {contact email}. We honour removal requests within 5 business days and can block that provider name from future imports.

## Applications

There is no in-app messaging. Providers see an applicant's profile and CV only after that applicant applies, and lose access 90 days after the job closes.

## Billing

If you cancel during the trial, jobs stay live until the trial end date. If payment fails, Stripe retries automatically. After a 3-day grace period unpaid jobs are unpublished (not deleted). Paying again republishes them with remaining days intact. Cancelled paid plans stay live until the end of the paid period.

## Acceptable use

Do not post misleading jobs, scrape the site, or upload content you do not have rights to. We may hide jobs after reports and suspend accounts that break these terms.
