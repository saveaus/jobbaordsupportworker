This privacy policy is a working draft. It will be replaced after legal review.

{Site name} ("we") is an Australian job board for support work. We collect only what we need to run the service.

## What we collect

- Account details: name, email, phone, postcode, business name and ABN.
- Applicant profile: work types, requirements, a short about-me, and a CV.
- Job listings and applications, including optional messages.
- Billing details are handled by Stripe. We store Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, not full card numbers.
- Technical data needed to run the site (a hashed visitor cookie for job-view counts, IP addresses for rate limiting).

## How we use it

- To show jobs, send applications, and email alerts you asked for.
- To bill providers and send transactional email.
- To moderate listings and prevent abuse.

## Who can see a CV and profile

A CV and profile are visible only to providers the applicant has applied to. Providers lose access 90 days after the job closes.

## Imported listings

We may list a publicly advertised job as a link to the original ad. Providers can claim the listing or ask us to remove it. Removal requests are honoured within 5 business days. Contact {contact email}.

## Overseas disclosure

Some processors (email, payments, hosting) may store data outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to keep it protected, consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your account from Settings. To make a complaint, email {contact email}. You may also contact the OAIC.
